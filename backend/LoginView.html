Privacy Notice & Communication Preferences

Please review and acknowledge the current Pattanabhumi Biology Privacy Notice before accessing your portal.

1. Information We Collect to Support Your Learning

Pattanabhumi Biology collects your student profile (username, full name, and email address), course enrollments, assessment progress, tuition payment records, uploaded transfer slips, and class absence notices so we can deliver your biology courses, verify tuition payments, and keep your class schedule up to date.

2. How Your Information Is Stored

Your student records, uploaded course handouts, and payment slips are stored securely in the school’s private cloud storage and are accessible only to you and authorized school instructors and administrators.

3. Sign-In Sessions & Browser Storage

  • Standard Sign-In: When you sign in without checking "Remember me", your session stays active only while your browser tab is open.
  • "Remember Me" (Optional): If you check "Remember me", your browser securely saves your sign-in session for up to 7 days on this device so you do not have to sign in every time.
  • No Advertising or Tracking Cookies: This learning portal does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers, and never stores your password or banking details in browser cookies.

4. Students Under 20 & Parent/Guardian Consent

If you are under 20 years of age, please review this notice together with your parent or legal guardian in accordance with school enrollment policies and the Thai Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

Parent / Guardian Notice for Students Under 20

Students under 20 should review this Privacy Notice with a parent or legal guardian. Optional school news emails are completely separate—you do not need to opt into marketing emails to access your courses or exams.
